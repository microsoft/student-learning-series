Case Study | Spring 2025

Campus Recycling Dashboard

Turning two years of campus waste data into a clear story and action plan.

This project used simulated educational data from January 2023 to December 2024 (24 monthly records) to understand waste trends and propose practical behavior-change pilots for campus sustainability teams.

What the project was

I created a dashboard-style analysis of landfill, recycling, compost, total tons, diversion rate, and contamination rate using the campus-waste-data-2023-2024-simulated.csv dataset and project charts.

Source files used: Campus Recycling Source Paper (DOCX / PDF), campus dataset CSV, and chart exports from the shared Campus Recycling source pack. All values are labeled simulated educational data for training/demo purposes.

What I did

Data review and trend analysis

  • Summarized year-over-year performance from 2023 to 2024.
  • Compared diversion and contamination trajectories month by month.
  • Checked whether landfill reductions held while total material volume changed.

Visual storytelling

  • Built and presented three supporting visuals: diversion trend, stream totals, and contamination trend.
  • Used plain-language chart captions so non-technical campus teams could use the findings quickly.

Action recommendations

  • Standardize tri-bin stations and signage in high-traffic areas.
  • Run monthly contamination-focused communication prompts and examples.
  • Publish simple building-level waste snapshots to reinforce behavior change.

The result

Key outcomes from the source data

  • Diversion rate improved from 49.2% in 2023 to 56.9% in 2024.
  • Landfill tonnage dropped from 930.0 to 807.5 tons.
  • Average contamination decreased from 17.4% to 14.2%.

Project impact

The dashboard and recommendations gave the sustainability office a concise baseline and three pilot-ready interventions, which supported implementation discussions for campus recycling improvements.
